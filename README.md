## Design inspiration

This theme is inspired by the visual identity and interior design of the **Normandy SR-2** from BioWare's *Mass Effect* series.

Its color palette and UI styling draw inspiration from:
- The Normandy SR-2's interior lighting, dark metallic surfaces, and technical panels.
- The visual language of Systems Alliance technology and military interfaces.

**This is an interpretation, not a direct recreation of the ship's in-game color scheme.** The palette has been adapted for Discord readability, visual consistency, and everyday use.
